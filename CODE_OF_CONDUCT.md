# Code of Conduct

Postmodem is software for communities, and we want its own community to be one people are
glad to join. This applies to everyone who takes part: in issues, pull requests,
discussions, reviews, and anywhere else you speak for the project.

## What we expect

- Be welcoming and patient, especially with newcomers and with people writing in a
  language that isn't their first. Postmodem has English and Polish users, and either
  language is fine.
- Criticise ideas and code, not people. Explain the why when you disagree.
- Accept that maintainers make the final call on scope and design, and that "not now" is a
  valid answer.
- Credit other people's work, and say so when you are wrong.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments about anyone's identity, background or
  ability.
- Sexualised language or imagery, threats, and intimidation.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption: trolling, flooding, or reopening settled discussions on purpose.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems privately to the maintainers at
**[conduct@a87.pl](mailto:conduct@a87.pl)**.
Reports are kept confidential, and the person who reported will not be identified to the
person reported without their consent.

Maintainers may respond with a private warning, by removing content, or by temporarily or
permanently banning someone from the project's spaces, depending on how serious and how
repeated the behaviour is. Maintainers who don't follow this code can be removed from the
project's maintainers.
