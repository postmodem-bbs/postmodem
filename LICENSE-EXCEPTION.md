only through the interfaces published in the `@postmodem/sdk` package (and its
sub-packages), the documented HTTP APIs, or the documented add-on bridge, the
copyright holders of Postmodem grant you permission to license and distribute
that Extension under terms of your choice, and to load and run it together with
Postmodem, without the Extension being considered a work based on Postmodem.
